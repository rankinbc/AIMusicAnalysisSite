# SPECTR — the flywheel

> From the 2026-06-16 brainstorming session. How library / analyze / listen / publish / react / taste-match wire into one self-reinforcing machine, and the order it has to spin up in.

**One line:** A bedroom producer's home base where you make your track better *because* people are going to hear it — and the people who hear it are producers like you.

The analyzer is the **hook**. The crowd + the data + the relationships are the **moat**.

---

## The surfaces (nodes)

| Surface | What it does | Produces | Credits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Library** | home base — every song + version + reference | the on-ramp (1 click to analyze or publish) | — |
| **Analyze** | pattern detector → known-mistake/indicator detection → findings → Moves → Game Plan (.md) | findings, Moves, a taste fingerprint | *spend* |
| **Listen** | the *alive* DSP sandbox — audition a Move before committing; also the live stage | a tried/kept fix | — |
| **Publish** | flip a version public → enters the async feed + can be queued to the live room | a heard track | *spend* (boost/DJ) |
| **React** | async: timestamped 😴🔥👎 + comments on the timeline · live: stage reactions, light-show, chat | timestamped crowd sentiment | *earn* (give good feedback) |
| **Taste-match** | uses the analysis fingerprint to pair producers who sound alike → follow, collab, seed each other's crowds | connections | — |

---

## The three loops

**1. Craft loop (solo — spins with ZERO other users):**
Library → Analyze → Listen (audition) → your DAW → re-analyze.
*The improvement engine. Useful as a pure tool today; this is the always-valid baseline and the hook.*

**2. Exposure loop (async — bootstraps from ONE listener):**
Publish → Crowd reacts (timestamped) → reactions become **new findings** ("12 humans bored @2:14") + **motivation** → back to Analyze/Listen/fix → re-publish.
*The motivation engine. Knowing people will hear it is the reason to sweat the mix. And the crowd is ground-truth feedback the AI literally cannot fake.*

**3. Connection loop (needs a few users):**
Analysis fingerprint → taste-match → producers listen/react/collab → denser crowds → more reactions → better taste data.
*The belonging engine and the network-effect moat. "Find the one person who sounds like you" in a sea of sameness.*

---

## The two layers that wire it all together

**Data substrate (one asset, three jobs):** the analysis output simultaneously (a) powers your private Game Plan, (b) is the *fingerprint* for taste-matching, and (c) is where crowd reactions flow back in as findings. Every analysis + every reaction sharpens all three. It compounds.

**Credit bloodstream:** *spend* to analyze / publish-boost / DJ a live set / fire reactions ⇄ *earn* by giving quality feedback on others' tracks. This is the reciprocity engine (solves "everyone wants feedback, nobody gives it") AND the monetization, in one system. (Credits/entitlements already built.)

## How it spins up (cold-start order — the critical part)

The machine is designed to be valuable at **every** size, so you never hit an empty-room death spiral:

1. **1 user (you):** craft loop only. Analyzer + Listen + library = a useful solo tool *with no crowd required*.
2. **A few users:** async Publish + comment/react. Bootstraps from a *single* listener — no "everyone online at once" requirement. Seed it yourself + a few producer Discords. Taste-match starts pairing.
3. **Density (later):** the live room. The spike. Only light it when enough producers are around to make a room fun. **Never lead with it.**

Each stage is valuable alone and pulls the next.

---

## Why it compounds (the moat, finally)

- Every analysis sharpens the genre/taste data.
- Every reaction adds ground-truth feedback + taste signal.
- Every published track + collaboration is switching cost + belonging.
- The crowd — real producers reacting at timestamps — is the one thing TrackScore / Slapback / RoEx **structurally cannot copy** without the same people.

## The one thing this map does NOT solve

Getting the first ~50 producers in the door. The flywheel spins beautifully *once moving* — the map doesn't move it from rest. That's distribution, and it's still the boss fight. (The saving grace: the craft loop gives people a reason to show up *before* the crowd exists.)

---

## Brand soul (so the build doesn't go corporate again)

Irreverent, fun, honest, for people making *weird, unique* music — not money-chasing generic trash. Anonymous by default (kills fear-of-judgment, makes the roast safe, attracts braver music). The coach can be blunt and a little funny. Every surface should feel *alive* like the Listen/DJ page, never like an earnest engineering report.
