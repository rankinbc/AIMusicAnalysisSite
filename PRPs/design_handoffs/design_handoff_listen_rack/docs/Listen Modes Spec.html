Three Modes:
Work, View, Room

The Listen page does three different jobs depending on why you opened it. Rather than one crowded surface, it adapts into three modes — a focused workbench, an async review room, and a live session — each exposing only the controls that serve its goal. This document captures the intent and the per-mode differences; a few decisions are deliberately left open at the end.

Work

The workbench

One person improving a track — analyse, tweak the rack, A/B, decide what to fix.

Who

Tense

State

Someone plays a track — their own or another's — and leaves feedback or suggestions.

01

Work — the workbench

The goal is singular: figure out how to make the track better, and do it. Everything social is gone. The page leans all the way into analysis and the rack, with the Coach as a second pair of ears. This is the only mode that is private by default.

What's front and centre

  • The rack, full-depth — every module expanded, drag-reorder, ⌁ Bindings, A/B Bypass, Reset. All three layouts (Inline / Console / Studio) available.
  • Coach + Plan — ask about the mix, get ranked, applyable fixes ("low end is heavy → try a 2 dB cut at 120 Hz"). The core "determine improvements" loop lives here.
  • Metering & Stats — LUFS-S, True Peak, RMS, correlation kept visible; full track analysis on hand for technical decisions.
  • Versions & presets — save the rack state as named iterations and A/B between them or against a reference track.
  • Private notes — to-fix markers dropped on the timeline, working notes only the author sees.

What's hidden or dialled down

  • Visuals console collapses to a utility monitor (spectrum + meters) — a working scope, not a light show.
  • People, live chat, reactions, presence — all absent. No audience.
  • No comments, bookmarks, or control-handoff — there's no one else in the room.
02

View — the review

A track gets played back for feedback, asynchronously. The viewer might be the author re-listening, or an invited reviewer. The defining surface is the comment & suggestion layer, and what's allowed is governed by the uploader's settings.

Feedback & comments

  • Timestamped comments — anchored to a point on the waveform/scrubber, so feedback lands exactly where it's about ("vocal sits too low @ 1:24").
  • General comments — track-level notes that aren't tied to a moment.
  • Comments are visible to the author and (optionally) other viewers; the author can resolve, pin, or hide.

Suggested rack presets

  • A reviewer can audition the rack locally (their changes never touch the author's master) and attach the result as a suggested preset on a comment — "try this chain."
  • The author can preview a suggestion A/B against their own, then accept it (how a merge works — full replace vs. cherry-pick — is an open question).

Bookmarking

  • If the uploader enables bookmarking, a viewer can save the track (and optionally a timestamp) to their own library.
  • Whether the author sees who bookmarked is a sharing/privacy decision left open below.

What the viewer sees of the mix

  • The author's rack and analysis are visible but read-only until the viewer forks it into a suggestion.
  • Visuals are personal and optional — nothing is synchronised between viewers.
03

Room — the session

The party mode: several people listening live, together, reacting in the moment. A host drives playback and the visuals for everyone; the room responds. This is where the visuals console becomes the star and feedback is fast and emotional rather than precise.

Live & shared

  • One host, many listeners — the host controls transport and the visual show; everyone sees the same stage in sync.
  • Presence & status — listener avatars with live status emoji; the host is badged.
  • Reactions & chat — a live reaction feed, floating emoji, and presence pops on the stage; a drop triggers a wave of reactions.

The show

  • Full visuals console — stages, lasers, colour sync, and the Auto-Program / Auto-React director driving the room hands-free.
  • Control handoff — the host can grant rack and visuals control to a listener independently (already built).

Dialled down

  • Coach, Plan and detailed metering recede — this isn't the moment for analysis.
  • The session is ephemeral; an optional recap (reaction timeline, hottest moments) can seed an async View thread afterwards.

05

Switching & transitions

The mode switcher replaces today's SOLO ⇄ ROOM segmented control in the header — now three segments. Availability is role-gated: View needs the track to be shared, Room needs you to be host or invited. The modes form a natural lifecycle:

Work — publish for feedback → View — go live → Room — session recap → View
  • Work → View — the author opens the track to reviewers. Private working notes stay private; the track, analysis and rack become viewable.
  • View → Room — a host starts a synchronous session and invited viewers join the live stage.
  • Room → View — when the session ends, the live reaction timeline and chat condense into an async thread; the hottest moments become timestamped comments.
06

Uploader permissions & sharing

What a viewer can do in View and Room is set by the uploader when they share a track. These gates determine which controls even render:

  • Visibility — Private (Work only) · Unlisted (link) · Public.
  • Comments — on / off, and who may comment (anyone with the link vs. named accounts).
  • Suggested presets — allow reviewers to propose rack chains, or not.
  • Bookmarking — let viewers save the track to their library, or not.
  • Live sessions — who can host a Room and who can join.
07

Deliberately left open

Decisions to make later

  1. Suggestion merge model — when an author accepts a suggested preset, is it a full rack replace or a cherry-pick of individual modules/params?
  2. Coach access in View — can reviewers run the Coach on someone else's track, or is it author-only?
  3. Anonymous viewers — may anon users leave comments / suggestions / bookmarks, or are those named-account only?
  4. Bookmark signal — is bookmarking a private save, or does the author see who/how many bookmarked?
  5. Room recap — automatic or opt-in, and which moments convert into View comments?
  6. Versions vs. presets — is a saved rack preset the same thing as a track version (v1/v2/v3), or separate concepts that relate?
  7. Notifications — where and how the author is told about new comments and suggestions.
  8. Work privacy — does Work ever surface any social signal, or stay strictly private until explicitly published?
